Quick answers before you book.

Where exactly is POP? +

34 W 32nd St, Floor 9 — right in the middle of Koreatown. Take the elevator up to 9.

Can groups reserve private rooms? +

Yes — book online for standard rooms, or send your date, arrival time, guest count, and package needs by phone or email for bigger plans.

How late are you open? +

Late every night — until 3 AM Sun–Wed, 4 AM Thursday, and 4:30 AM Friday and Saturday.
